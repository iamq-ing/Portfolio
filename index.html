· PERSONAL ESSAY
I love the exact moment when a mechanical frame wakes up with its first line of code.I believe that robotics starts where software ends— at the edge of the rift that separates physicality from programming.
Scientists study the world as it is; engineers create the world that has never been.— Theodore von Kármán
Good robotics is never a solo project; it thrives on shared late-night soldering and collaborative problem solving. I aspire to be someone who eases through both.
I believe that technology exists to ease the human brain, not to replace it. The true potential of multiple software technologies can only be harnessed with hardware that works on it. Robots and machinery make the world easier.We must design hardware that uplifts our community. Instead of generating meme images via AI, I believe using it to perform hard tasks that endanger human lives is much more significant. And this is something that can only be done via hardware.
